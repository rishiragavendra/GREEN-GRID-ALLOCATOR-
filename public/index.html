📘 Guide

What is this?

A machine-learning driven grid dispatcher. Three traditional machine-learning regression models (trained on Tetuan City's 2017 hourly data) forecast demand, solar and wind output from weather, cyclical time, lag and rolling-mean features. Every hour the allocator dispatches your sources to meet demand as cleanly as possible. The Model Performance panel shows the live benchmark of every algorithm that was compared.

How dispatch works

  1. Renewables first — solar, wind & hydro run at full output (zero cost, zero CO₂).
  2. Battery — surplus clean energy charges the battery (90% round-trip); a deficit makes it discharge.
  3. Fossil last — gas, coal & diesel cover whatever is still missing, cheapest first.

💡 Surplus that the battery can't absorb is curtailed — shown on the cards. A bigger battery means less curtailment.

Controls

  • Time — ±1h steps, a date/time picker to jump anywhere, or ▶ auto-play.
  • Demand — ±10 MW or type an exact value.
  • Weather — change temperature / humidity / wind; leave a field blank to keep its current value.
  • Add / Remove — the dock's ➕ Add adds any source; each table row has an ✕ to remove it.
  • Save / Load — persist & restore the grid to grid_state.json.
  • Export / Import — download the grid as JSON or upload one back.
