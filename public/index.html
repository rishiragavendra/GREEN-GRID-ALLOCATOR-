      How to read these forecasts

      • Solar and wind are predicted as capacity factors (0 to 1) from weather and time of day. The simulator multiplies each by the installed capacity.
      • Training targets are synthetic. The dataset has no measured renewable output, so solar and wind targets are derived from weather readings. Their high R² shows the models learned that relationship. It is not evidence of real-world forecast skill.
      • Demand is a scenario input. You set it with the demand controls. The demand forecasting model is benchmarked on the Model Performance page but does not drive dispatch.
      • History is session-only. The chart records hours you simulate in this browser. Nothing is stored on the server.

      Dispatch rules

      1. Renewables first. Solar, wind and hydro run at full available output.
      2. Battery. Surplus charges storage at 90% round-trip efficiency. A shortfall discharges it.
      3. Fossil last. Gas, coal and diesel fill what remains, cheapest first.

      Energy the battery cannot absorb is reported as curtailed. Demand the grid cannot cover is reported as unmet. Indicator definitions: Supply coverage is the share of demand met. Clean energy utilisation is the share of renewable output that is not curtailed.
